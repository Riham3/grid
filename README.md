# Riham Blogs' Photo Collage Maker
**Try it out at:** [https://cigarettesprettysmokes.pages.dev/grid/](https://cigarettesprettysmokes.pages.dev/grid/)
 
## Overview
Design custom photo grid layouts, adjust spacing, use textures, and export in high-resolution. 
 
It doesn't restrict you to ready-made templates, you can build your own flexible layout with up to ten rows and columns. 
 
Report bugs or request features on Riham Blogs' [Submit Feedback](https://cigarettesprettysmokes.pages.dev/feedback/).
 
 
## Template
Click a slot to select it. Select two or more adjacent slots to merge them, as long as they form a rectangle.
 
Click **Merge** to combine them and **Split** to break a merged slot back into individual ones.
 
Drag a divider to resize the neighboring slots together. The total layout size remains the same as space between them is redistributed. Slots can't shrink below a minimum. 
 
Updating the rows or columns resets the layout to its original state. Click **Reset** to undo merges and resizes instantly.
 
 
## Preview
The Preview tab is where you insert images into your layout and shape the final output. 
 
Pick an aspect ratio for your grid: Square (1:1), Portrait (4:5), Widescreen (16:9), or Vertical (9:16). The canvas reshapes instantly, and the content reflows proportionally so your layout stays consistent across ratios.
 
Padding sets the space between the canvas edge and the outer slots, gap sets the space between slots, and border radius rounds the corners of the canvas and every slot. 
 
Choose a background/texture behind your photos. Select **Solid** to use a custom flat color. 
 
Click on any slot to insert an image. Click a slot with an existing image to replace it. Files must be under **10MB** and are processed locally without the use of any external server. 
 
Images are placed at their natural aspect ratio and can be repositioned and scaled inside their frame. 
 
Click and drag to move the image. Scroll or pinch to zoom. The image is clipped to its frame. 
 
Click **Export** to download as an image file. The rounded corners are preserved as transparent. 
 
Modifying the template in any way clears the uploaded images. The customization remains exactly as it is.
